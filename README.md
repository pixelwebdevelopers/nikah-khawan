### Brand Color Palette

Use these colors consistently throughout the entire website:

- **Royal Purple:** `#370858` — primary brand color for headings, selected buttons, icons, navigation accents, and important elements.
- **Elegant Gold:** `#D9A62E` — accent color for buttons, borders, icons, dividers, decorative elements, and highlights.
- **Champagne Gold:** `#F2D17A` — secondary gold for subtle highlights, hover effects, and refined decorative details.
- **Soft Lavender:** `#F0E8FB` — a gentle background color for selected website sections.
- **Pearl White:** `#FBF9FF` — a warm, premium off-white background for alternating sections.
- **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` — use for important sections that need a clean, bright, minimal appearance.
- **Deep Charcoal:** `#17121D` — primary body text for readability.

### Important: Background Balance

I do NOT want the website to use too much dark purple. The overall website should feel bright, spacious, welcoming, sophisticated, and premium.

Use a balanced combination of white, pearl white, and soft lavender backgrounds throughout the website.

- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Use for sections such as the main hero content area, service details, booking forms, testimonials, and other content-heavy areas where clarity and cleanliness are important.
- **Pearl White (`#FBF9FF`):** Use for alternating sections, informational content, FAQs, and subtle background variations.
- **Soft Lavender (`#F0E8FB`):** Use selectively for the services section, process steps, feature highlights, and other sections that benefit from a gentle purple tint.
- **Royal Purple (`#370858`):** Use sparingly for the footer, selected call-to-action sections, small navigation accents, important headings, and primary buttons. Avoid making every section or large background dark purple.
- **Gold (`#D9A62E`):** Use as a refined accent, not as a dominant background color. Apply it to button borders, small icons, separators, checkmarks, and elegant decorative details.

### Suggested Section Layout

1. **Header:** White or pearl-white background, purple logo/text, dark-purple navigation text, and subtle gold accents.
2. **Hero Section:** Primarily white or pearl white, with purple typography, gold highlights, and attractive wedding/Nikah imagery. Avoid a full dark-purple hero background.
3. **Our Services:** Soft lavender background with white service cards, purple headings, and small gold icons or borders.
4. **Why Choose Us:** Pure white background with purple headings, charcoal text, and elegant gold checkmarks.
5. **How It Works:** Pearl-white background with a clean step-by-step layout and subtle gold connecting lines.
6. **Wedding/Nikah Gallery:** Pure white background with high-quality images, clean spacing, and minimal gold accents.
7. **Testimonials:** Soft lavender background with white testimonial cards, purple headings, and subtle gold stars or decorative elements.
8. **FAQ Section:** Pure white or pearl-white background with clean accordion elements and purple accents.
9. **Booking / Contact CTA:** A light background with purple typography and a prominent purple button with white text and a subtle gold detail. A small purple panel is acceptable, but avoid an oversized dark section.
10. **Footer:** Royal-purple background is acceptable here, with white text, champagne-gold icons, and gold dividers.

### Design Style

The visual direction should communicate trust, elegance, professionalism, and the cultural significance of Nikah services.

- Use a modern, premium, minimal design rather than an overly decorative traditional template.
- Use elegant typography with excellent support for Urdu and English if Urdu content is included.
- Use generous whitespace and consistent section spacing.
- Use subtle gold borders, fine-line ornaments, and small decorative accents inspired by the reference design.
- Use tasteful wedding photography and authentic imagery relevant to Nikah ceremonies in Pakistan.
- Use rounded corners moderately and keep shadows soft and subtle.
- Ensure buttons, text, and backgrounds have sufficient contrast and remain accessible.
- Keep the overall design cohesive, with alternating white, pearl-white, and soft-lavender sections to create visual rhythm.

### Color Usage Rules

The website should be predominantly light. Aim for approximately 70–80% light backgrounds (white, pearl white, and soft lavender), with purple used mainly for branding, typography, buttons, and a limited number of dark elements. Gold should remain a restrained accent.

Do not use neon colors, overly saturated purple gradients, excessive gold, or dark-purple backgrounds across multiple consecutive sections.

**Final goal:** Create a polished, trustworthy, premium-looking Nikah Khawan website that preserves the royal-purple-and-gold brand identity while feeling bright, modern, airy, and elegant. The colors should complement the content and photography rather than overpower them.
